Big Tech has sold us the illusion that artificial intelligence is a
frictionless technology that will bring wealth and prosperity to humanity. But
hidden beneath this smooth surface lies the grim reality of a precarious global
workforce of millions that labour under often appalling conditions to make AI
possible.

Based on hundreds of interviews and thousands of hours of fieldwork over more
than a decade, this book shows us the lives of the workers often deliberately
concealed from view and the systems of power that determine their future.
