        in our cabs. The problem is, these thermometers max out at 40 degrees

        who are bringing in their own thermometers are reporting in-cab
        temperatures up to 50 degrees. Work becomes unbearable. Drivers are
        having to pull over to douse themselves in water. It’s illegal to
        transport livestock in temperatures over 30 degrees, and yet we’re meant
        to keep driving buses in temperatures way beyond that. They’re asking us
        to do a job that’s physically impossible.”
